# Free art plan (no spending)

**Status:** approved, and the packs are downloaded. **Cost: $0.**
**Checked:** 22 November 2026.

Pytt doesn't want to spend money yet. This plan gets the best look possible for free, using only art that is legally free to use in a commercial game (one we sell on Steam).

## The licence: CC0

Everything picked here uses **CC0 ("public domain")**. That means:
- It's free to use, including in a game we sell.
- No credit is required (we'll credit the makers anyway, as a thank-you).
- We can change it however we like and keep it in our GitHub project.

The packs are "name your own price" on itch.io, and **$0 is accepted**.

## One matching style

The two makers below, **KayKit** (Kay Lousberg) and **Quaternius**, both make chunky, colourful, cartoon low-poly art. Their packs look good together, which matters more than any single pack looking great.

### The Bastion Golem

I didn't find a free, animated stone golem that's good enough. Instead, we **build our own Bastion Golem from the free rock and ruin pieces**: a giant made of stacked stone blocks and pillars with glowing runes, animated with code. This suits our design anyway. A golem made of ruined castle stone is more original than a store-bought one, and it can be 15 m tall without looking stretched.

## Free look upgrades (no art needed)

These make *everything* look better, including the current shapes, and cost nothing:
- **Lighting:** a warmer sun, soft sky light and gentle fog for depth, toward the warm, painterly feel of *How To Train Your Dragon*.
- **Colour grading and bloom:** richer colours, and glowing runes and spells that really glow.
- **A painted gradient sky** instead of the default grey-blue.
- **Toon-style outlines** on characters, so they pop against the background.

## Honest expectations

This art is cute and chunky, more "stylised indie game" than "animated film". It's a big step up from the clay shapes and good enough to show friends and playtest. Later, if the game proves fun, we can move to paid packs or a custom artist for the signature creatures. The game code stays the same either way.

## How we get the files

The packs are free downloads on itch.io. Two ways:
1. **I download them for you** using the Claude app's browser on your Mac. You might be asked to allow access to itch.io once.
2. **You download them:** on each pack page, click **Download Now**, then **"No thanks, just take me to the downloads"**, then download the zip files. Tell me when they're in your Downloads folder.

Either way, I then bring them into the game and replace the shapes.

## What we found after downloading (22 November 2026)

- **Downloaded, all $0 and CC0:** KayKit Adventurers 2.0 (free), KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0 (free), KayKit Dungeon Pack 1.1 (free), Quaternius LowPoly Animated Monsters, and the Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit (standard).
- **Fewer animations than advertised.** The free Adventurers 2.0 tier has about 25 animations (idle, walk, run, jump, hit, death, throw, spawn and a few more), but **no sword or spell attacks**. So the attacks are animated by code: the arm follows the same timed swing arcs the combat already uses. This keeps the rhythm timing exact.
- **No goblin in the monster pack.** It has a slime, a bat, a skeleton and a small dragon. So the goblins are made from KayKit adventurers instead: shrunk, with green skin, a bigger head and long pointed ears, holding a dagger (Burrowers) or throwing rocks (Slingers). The monster pack is saved for later creatures. Its zip has no licence file, so its CC0 status rests on the download page.
- **KayKit Character Animations** turned out to be an older pack for the previous character version, so it was skipped.
- **The Nature MegaKit is 117 MB**, mostly large textures. It's kept on the Mac for later and isn't in the project yet; the KayKit forest pieces cover the first slice.
