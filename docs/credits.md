| Pack | Maker | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| KayKit Character Pack: Adventurers 2.0 (free) | Kay Lousberg, [kaylousberg.com](https://www.kaylousberg.com) | CC0 1.0 | The Knight (Sword) and Mage heroes, their weapons and animations, and the reshaped goblins |
| KayKit Forest Nature Pack 1.0 (free) | Kay Lousberg | CC0 1.0 | Trees, bushes, grass and rocks |
| KayKit Dungeon Pack 1.1 (free) | Kay Lousberg | CC0 1.0 | Ruins, floor tiles, torches and props |

The original licence files are kept next to the art, in `Sealbound/Assets/Resources/Art/KayKit/<pack>/License.txt`.
