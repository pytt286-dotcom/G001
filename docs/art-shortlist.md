## How to judge these

1. **Open each link and look at the pictures and videos.** I can read prices and version details, but I can't see the artwork, so only you can judge the look.
2. **Try to keep one style.** Packs from different makers can clash, for example a chunky cartoon hero next to a realistic goblin. Look for a similar shape style and colour feel.
3. **Set expectations.** Ready-made packs are a big step up from the clay shapes, but they won't reach *How To Train Your Dragon* quality. That level needs a custom artist later (see the design document, section 11). These packs are for proving the game is fun.
4. **Animations.** For human-shaped characters, I can add free animations from Adobe Mixamo (running, sword swings and more). Creatures like the golem need their own animations, so for those packs check that the listing says "animated".

## How buying works

1. Open the pack's link, sign in with your Unity account (the same one you use in Unity Hub), and click **Buy now** or **Add to cart**. Pay as normal.
2. The packs then appear under **My Assets** in your Unity account.
3. Tell me which packs you bought. I'll import them into the project on your Mac, replace the clay shapes, and make sure everything still works.
